FAQs & delivery

How long does delivery take?

Orders in Nairobi usually arrive within 1–3 days. Countrywide delivery is sent by courier. We confirm timing on WhatsApp.

How do I pay?

We accept M-Pesa. Message us on WhatsApp to confirm your order and get payment details.

Are your products genuine?

Yes. We sell original products and every item comes with a warranty.

How do I place an order?

Add items to your cart and press "Checkout on WhatsApp". Your order is sent to us and we confirm it with you.

What if an item is faulty?

Contact us right away with your order details and we will help with a repair, replacement or refund according to the warranty.
